The QA gap

Critical testing gets squeezed.

Business Central projects combine standard Microsoft behavior with extensions, integrations, migrated data, reports, permissions, and company-specific workflows. Manual testing rarely keeps pace with that surface area.

01 / COVERAGE

The happy path is not enough

Teams test what they remember. Edge cases, role differences, calculations, and downstream documents are easy to miss.

02 / CAPACITY

Experts become the bottleneck

The people who understand the workflow are already responsible for implementation, training, support, and go-live.

03 / EVIDENCE

“It failed” is not a defect report

Developers need exact steps, expected results, actual results, screenshots, and affected records—not a vague message.

Deployment

Start narrow. Prove value. Expand.

The best first target is a workflow that matters, repeats often, has clear expected results, and currently consumes expert testing time.

Select the workflow

Identify the business process, current risks, environment, users, data, and success criteria.

Teach and instrument

Document the workflow, build test cases, configure tools, and establish evidence requirements.

Run a supervised pilot

Execute against approved data, review findings with experts, and refine unreliable steps.

Operationalize

Hand off the runbook, schedule regression coverage, and expand to additional workflows.

Control stays with you

Useful automation with explicit boundaries.

A QA agent should increase test capacity without creating uncontrolled access or pretending uncertain results are facts.

Approved non-production environments

Testing begins in a designated sandbox with scoped access and known test records.

Human approval for consequential actions

Posting, external communication, production changes, and destructive operations remain controlled.

Evidence before conclusions

Results include the steps, records, calculations, screenshots, or documents that support the finding.

Known limits and stop conditions

The agent reports blockers and uncertainty rather than improvising beyond the approved workflow.
