AI FOR QA

Software testing is essential work. AI makes it affordable to do more of it.

In a Microsoft Business Central migration, TVC used AI-assisted QA to translate business workflows into executable test coverage: customer setup, customer-specific rates, prepaid and collect freight bills, manifesting, posting, printed documents, and report layout verification.

AI helped accelerate the repetitive parts: reading documentation, creating test data, driving browser workflows, checking rate math, logging results, and turning screenshots or PDFs into clear developer-ready defects.

Documented QA runs

RECENT WORK

AI-accelerated Azure cleanup that lowers spend.

For an established business running Azure infrastructure, TVC used AI-assisted analysis and hands-on Azure engineering to review virtual machines, backup vaults, storage accounts, WAF posture, and monthly cost drivers.

The work retired stale infrastructure, preserved recovery assets where needed, moved legacy backup archives to lower-cost storage, and produced a clear savings model tied to the company's business goals.
