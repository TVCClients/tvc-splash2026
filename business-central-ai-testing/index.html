Why testing breaks down

Business Central is standard software—until it isn’t.

Extensions, integrations, migrated data, permissions, reports, and company-specific rules create a testing surface that grows with every release. Manual testing depends on scarce experts remembering every step.

Important paths get missed

Teams cover the familiar happy path while exceptions, role differences, and downstream documents receive less attention.

Experts become the bottleneck

The people who understand the process are also handling configuration, training, support, and go-live preparation.

Defects arrive without evidence

Developers lose time recreating failures when reports omit the exact steps, records, expected result, and screenshots.

What the tester does

A repeatable QA process, not a chatbot.

The AI tester follows approved procedures in a controlled Business Central environment. It documents what happened and hands uncertain or consequential decisions back to people.

01

Executes workflows

02

03

Common questions

Control stays with your team.

The operating boundaries are part of the solution: approved environments, defined credentials, known test data, human review, and clear stop conditions.

Does the tester operate in production?

Testing starts in a designated sandbox or other approved non-production environment. Production access and consequential actions require explicit controls and human approval.

Is this an off-the-shelf testing product?

No. TVC configures the testing capability around your Business Central workflows, extensions, terminology, data boundaries, and expected outcomes.

Will it replace our functional experts?

No. Experts define correct behavior and review important findings. The tester increases their capacity by executing repeatable work and collecting evidence.

What does a pilot include?

A focused pilot typically includes one selected workflow, test design, approved data, supervised execution, evidence standards, findings review, and a recommendation for broader coverage.
